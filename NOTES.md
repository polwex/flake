
Known gaps in the app

- No end-to-end encryption. The server can read messages and voice notes. You've decided that comes later.
- One device per account. Signing in on a second phone logs out the first.
- No history sync. Signing in on a new phone brings back the account and contacts who write to you, but not old messages.
- No read receipts, typing indicator, or unread counts.
- No photos or files. It would reuse the voice-note upload path, so it's relatively cheap to add.
- No group chats.
- No settings screen: you can't change your display name, and there's no account deletion or app version display.
- Debug builds only: about 120 MB, signed with the project's debug key. Release builds come at the release step.

Suggested next steps

1. Turn on notifications on your phone, and deploy the passkey fingerprint. Then test passkeys for real: tap Add on the card, uninstall, reinstall, and sign in with the passkey. That's the one feature not yet tested end to end.
2. Photos and files. Probably the most noticeable missing feature for friends and family.
3. Unread counts and read receipts. Small, but they make chatting feel alive.
4. A settings screen: change your name, see the app version, delete your account.
